### The Sleezy Framework
Our proprietary "Sleezy" agentics framework isn't about being underhanded—it's about being frictionless. By deploying specialized AI employees, we've automated the critical path of nightclub operations.

#### 1. Automated Lead Qualification
Instead of a promoter manually checking Instagram DMs, our AI agents qualify leads in real-time. They check past attendance, spending history, and social signals to prioritize high-value bookings instantly.

#### 2. VIP Guest List & Table Management
The 'Sleezy' agents manage the floor in real-time. They can adjust table assignments based on arrival times and party sizes, ensuring the club is always at peak efficiency without a manager needing to touch a spreadsheet.

#### 3. Real-Time Logistics
One of the biggest pain points in premium nightlife is "The Departure." Our system automatically coordinates with transport partners to ensure that as soon as a VIP is ready to leave, their ride is at the valet.
