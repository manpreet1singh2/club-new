# Account Strategist

## Overview
Land-and-expand account planning agent focused on QBRs, stakeholder mapping, and identifying expansion opportunities within existing accounts.

## Core Responsibilities
- Generate account expansion plans
- Prepare QBR (Quarterly Business Review) presentations
- Map organizational stakeholders and decision-makers
- Monitor account health signals

## Tech Stack
- Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet
- Night Crew Sales Dashboard
- Notion for account plans
- Gmail for stakeholder communication
